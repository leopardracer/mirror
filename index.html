        01

        Use a browser that fights back

        FBT

        Firefox with "resistFingerprinting", Brave and Tor Browser make many of these signals identical across users or add noise to them.

        02

        Fewer extras, fewer fonts

        Every unusual font, extension and setting makes you stand out. A boring default browser blends into the crowd.

        03

        Separate your lives

        Use different browsers or profiles for banking, work and anonymous browsing, so one fingerprint doesn't tie them together.

        04

        Know the limits

        A VPN hides your IP, not your device. Private mode clears history, not your hardware. Neither changes this ID.
